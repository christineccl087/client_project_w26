Career Planning Resources

This page brings together the core career planning resources offered by the UMSI Career Development Office to help you navigate internships, full-time roles, and long-term career goals. Whether you’re just starting to explore options or actively applying, these resources are designed to support you at every stage of your journey.


Career Coaching

Career coaching offers personalized, one-on-one support to help you clarify your goals and make confident decisions. Through coaching sessions, you can explore career paths, refine your job search strategy, practice interviews, or get feedback on resumes and portfolios. Coaches understand the diverse backgrounds and career outcomes of UMSI students and tailor their guidance to your unique interests and experience.

Whether you’re feeling uncertain, stuck, or just want a second perspective, career coaching is a valuable space to reflect, ask questions, and plan next steps with intention.

Career Resources

UMSI’s career resources provide practical tools you can return to throughout your time as a student. These include guides on resume and cover letter writing, interview preparation, networking strategies, salary negotiation, and career exploration. Many resources are organized by career stage or role type, making it easier to find what’s relevant to you right now.

Career Resources

UMSI CareerLink

CareerLink is UMSI’s central platform for job and internship postings, employer events, and career-related announcements. It connects students with opportunities curated specifically for UMSI programs and skill sets, including roles in UX, product, data, research, information science, and more.

Beyond job listings, CareerLink also helps you stay informed about career fairs, employer info sessions, and workshops, making it easier to engage with opportunities as they arise.

UMSI CareerLink

CDO Canvas Page

The Career Development Office Canvas page serves as a structured learning space for career planning. It includes recorded workshops, step-by-step modules, timelines, and curated resources you can work through at your own pace. This space is especially useful if you prefer a guided, self-directed approach to career preparation.

By regularly checking the Canvas page, you can stay up to date on new content, upcoming events, and additional resources tailored to UMSI students.
