Networking at UMSI

Networking is about building genuine connections, learning from others’ experiences, and discovering opportunities that may not appear in job postings. At UMSI, you have access to a strong ecosystem of alumni, peers, and career professionals who are eager to support your growth — whether you’re exploring career paths, seeking advice, or preparing for internships and full-time roles.

This page brings together trusted networking resources from the UMSI Career Development Office and the University of Michigan to help you network with confidence and purpose.

Career Development Office Canvas Page

The Career Development Office Canvas page offers structured guidance on networking fundamentals. You’ll find modules and resources that walk through how to reach out to professionals, write effective outreach messages, prepare for informational interviews, and follow up thoughtfully.

This space is especially helpful if you’re new to networking or want a clear framework you can return to as you practice and refine your approach over time.

These resources cover topics such as building professional relationships, attending networking events, preparing conversation starters, and maintaining long-term connections.

    Whether you’re networking online, at events, or one-on-one, these guides help you approach each interaction with clarity and professionalism.

    • University Career Alumni Network (UCAN)

    The University Career Alumni Network (UCAN) connects you with University of Michigan alumni who have volunteered to support current students. Through UCAN, you can explore alumni profiles, learn about their career paths, and reach out for informational interviews or advice.

    Because UCAN is built on a culture of mentorship, it’s a welcoming place to ask questions, gain insight into different industries, and learn how others navigated similar decisions and challenges.
